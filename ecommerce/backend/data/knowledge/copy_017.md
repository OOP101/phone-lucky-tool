# 售后道歉与补发邮件模板

## 邮件目的与核心信息
售后道歉与补发邮件旨在安抚客户情绪，提升品牌信任度。应在客户提出投诉或问题后 24 小时内发送。

## 常用邮件结构
1. **主题**：清晰表达歉意与解决方案，如《Apology & Replacement for Your Order # [No.]》
2. **称呼**：使用客户姓名或“Dear [Customer Name]”
3. **正文内容**：
   - 表达真诚歉意
   - 说明问题原因（如物流延误、产品质量问题等）
   - 提出解决方案（如补发、退款、折扣等）
   - 提供后续跟进方式
4. **结尾**：再次致谢并邀请客户反馈

## 示例模板
```
主题：Apology & Replacement for Your Order # [No.]

Dear [Customer Name],

We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience caused by the issue with your recent order # [Order No.]. We understand how frustrating this must be, and we want to assure you that we are taking immediate steps to resolve this.

To make things right, we will send a replacement product at no additional cost. The new item will be shipped within 3 business days. If you prefer a refund instead, please let us know, and we will process it immediately.

We truly value your trust and appreciate the opportunity to make this right. Please feel free to reach out to us at [Email/Phone] if you have any further concerns.

Thank you for your understanding and continued support.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Company Name]
```

## 注意事项
- 保持语气诚恳，避免推卸责任
- 明确解决方案，减少客户疑虑
- 后续跟进客户满意度
