# B2B 英文开发信模板

## 1. 邮件主题撰写规范
邮件主题需简洁明了，突出合作价值，建议控制在 60 字以内。

示例：
> Partnership Opportunity: Custom Manufacturing Services for Your E-commerce Business

## 2. 开发信正文结构
开发信应包含以下部分：问候语、自我介绍、合作价值、产品优势、请求行动、结尾礼貌用语。

> Dear [Client Name],

I hope this message finds you well. My name is [Your Name], and I am the sales manager at [Your Company Name], a leading manufacturer of [Product Category]. We specialize in providing high-quality, cost-effective solutions for international e-commerce businesses like yours.

We have noticed your business focuses on [Specific Product or Market], and we believe our custom manufacturing capabilities can significantly enhance your product offerings. Our products are certified by [Certification Name] and have been exported to over [Number] countries.

Would you be available for a brief call next week to discuss how we can support your growth? We would be happy to provide samples and detailed quotations upon request.

Thank you for your time, and I look forward to your response.

Best regards,
[Your Full Name]
[Your Position]
[Your Company Name]
[Contact Information]

## 3. 常见问题回复策略
针对客户可能提出的问题，提前准备简短回答，如价格、交货期、最小起订量等。

| 问题 | 回答 |
|------|------|
| What is your MOQ? | Minimum order quantity is 50 units per item. |
| How long is your delivery time? | Typically 15-20 days after payment confirmation. |
| Do you offer OEM services? | Yes, we support custom branding and packaging. |

## 4. 跟进策略建议
若客户未回复，可在 3-5 天后发送跟进邮件，内容简洁，重点突出合作价值。

示例：
> Dear [Client Name],

I just wanted to follow up on my previous email regarding potential collaboration opportunities. We believe our services could add significant value to your business, and we are eager to explore this further.

Please let me know if there is a convenient time to discuss this in more detail. Looking forward to your reply.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
