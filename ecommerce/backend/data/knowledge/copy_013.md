# 玩具品类英文 Listing 文案范例

## 1. 标题撰写规范
玩具类产品的英文标题需包含产品类型、适用年龄、功能特点、安全认证等信息，建议控制在 60 字以内。

示例：
> Educational Wooden Puzzle Toy - 12-Piece, Ages 3+, Safe & Non-Toxic, STEM Learning Kit

## 2. 产品卖点清单
列出 5-7 个核心卖点，突出教育性、安全性、趣味性等。

| 卖点 | 描述 |
|------|------|
| Educational Value | Enhances cognitive and motor skills through play |
| Safe Materials | Made from non-toxic, child-safe wood |
| Fun & Engaging | Colorful and interactive design for kids |
| Easy to Assemble | Simple instructions for quick setup |
| Durable Construction | Sturdy wooden pieces for long-term use |

## 3. 详细描述结构
详细描述应分段落，每段不超过 4 句话，内容包括产品特点、适用年龄、安全说明等。

> This wooden puzzle toy is designed for children aged 3 and above to develop their problem-solving and hand-eye coordination skills. It includes 12 colorful wooden pieces that are easy to assemble and disassemble. The non-toxic paint ensures safe play for young children. Ideal for home or classroom use, this toy promotes early learning and creativity.

## 4. 关键词优化建议
建议在标题、五点描述、商品详情页中合理分布关键词，避免堆砌。常见关键词包括：wooden puzzle, educational toy, kids' game, STEM learning, safe toy 等。

## 5. 图片与视频要求
产品图片需展示多角度细节，主图需清晰显示产品名称和玩法。建议提供 5 张以上高质量图片，并可添加 1-2 分钟的产品演示视频。

## 6. 法规与认证信息
如产品涉及欧盟 EN71 或美国 ASTM F963 认证，需在商品详情页明确标注。例如：
> EN71 Certified - Meets European safety standards for toys
