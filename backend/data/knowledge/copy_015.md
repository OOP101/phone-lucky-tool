# 外贸询盘回复话术模板

## 1. 回复邮件结构
外贸询盘回复邮件应包含以下部分：感谢客户、确认需求、提供解决方案、报价与条款、请求进一步沟通。

> Dear [Customer Name],

Thank you for your inquiry. We appreciate your interest in our [Product Name].

Based on your request, we would like to confirm the following details:
- Quantity: [Quantity] units
- Delivery time: [Delivery Time] days after payment
- Payment terms: [Payment Terms]

Our standard price is [Price] USD per unit, with a minimum order quantity of [MOQ] units. We also offer customized options upon request.

Please let us know if you need any additional information or samples. We are happy to assist you further.

Looking forward to your reply.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Your Position]
[Company Name]
[Contact Info]

## 2. 常见问题解答话术
针对客户可能提出的问题，提前准备标准回答，提高回复效率。

| 问题 | 回答 |
|------|------|
| Can you provide a sample? | Yes, we can send a free sample upon request. |
| What is your payment term? | We accept T/T, L/C, and other common methods. |
| What is your lead time? | Typically 15-20 days after payment confirmation. |

## 3. 报价单格式建议
报价单应包含产品名称、规格、数量、单价、总价、付款方式、交货时间等信息，建议使用表格形式。

| Item | Specification | Quantity | Unit Price (USD) | Total (USD) |
|------|-------------|----------|------------------|-------------|
| Product A | 10x10cm | 100 | 5.00 | 500.00 |
| Product B | 15x15cm | 50 | 8.00 | 400.00 |

## 4. 客户需求确认清单
在回复中建议客户提供以下信息以加快处理流程：
- 产品型号或规格
- 数量要求
- 交货时间要求
- 支付方式偏好
- 是否需要定制服务

## 5. 后续跟进建议
若客户未及时回复，可在 3-5 天后发送跟进邮件，内容简洁，重点突出合作价值。

示例：
> Dear [Customer Name],

I hope you are doing well. Just a gentle reminder about our previous email regarding your inquiry. We are ready to proceed with your order and would appreciate your feedback at your earliest convenience.

Feel free to reach out if you have any questions or need further assistance.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
