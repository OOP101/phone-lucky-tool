# 合规邀评站内信模板

## 邮件目的与核心信息
合规邀评站内信用于引导客户进行真实评价，符合平台规则（如 Amazon 的 Review Policy）。需在订单完成 7-14 天后发送。

## 常用邮件结构
1. **标题**：简明扼要，如《Your Feedback Matters - [Order No.]》
2. **称呼**：使用客户姓名或“Dear [Customer Name]”
3. **正文内容**：
   - 感谢客户购买
   - 引导客户进行评价（不强制）
   - 说明评价的重要性（如帮助其他买家决策）
   - 提供评价链接或指引
4. **结尾**：再次感谢并提供客服联系方式

## 示例模板
```
标题：Your Feedback Matters - [Order No.]

Dear [Customer Name],

Thank you for purchasing [Product Name] from us. We hope you are satisfied with your purchase.

We would greatly appreciate it if you could take a moment to leave a review on your order. Your feedback helps other customers make informed decisions and also helps us improve our service.

You can leave your review here: [Review Link].

If you have any questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us at [Email/Phone].

Thank you once again for choosing us!

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Company Name]
```

## 注意事项
- 不诱导好评，避免违规
- 使用中立、客观的语言
- 提供清晰的评价路径
